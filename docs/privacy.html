Flint — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026

Flint is a desktop email client developed by Devah Schaefers. It runs entirely on your own computer. Flint has no backend servers, and the developer never receives, stores, or has access to your data.

What data Flint accesses

When you connect a Gmail account, Flint asks for your consent via Google's OAuth screen and requests the following scopes:

  • gmail.modify — to read your messages and labels, and to apply the changes you make in the app (marking read/unread, starring, archiving, moving to trash or spam).
  • openid, email, profile — to identify which account you connected and show its address and name in the app.

Where your data is stored

All email data, account information, and OAuth tokens are stored locally on your device, in a database in the app's data folder. Nothing is transmitted anywhere except directly between your device and Google's APIs over encrypted connections. There are no analytics, no trackers, and no third-party services.

How your data is used

Your data is used solely to display and manage your email inside the app, at your direction. Flint does not sell, share, or transfer your data to anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.

Flint's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Removing an account inside Flint deletes that account's locally stored mail and tokens. You can also revoke Flint's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes everything.
